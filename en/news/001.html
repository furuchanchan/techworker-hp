We published our Generative AI Use Cases white paper

2026.03.20　Announcement

TechWorker Inc. today released a new white paper, "Generative AI Use Cases 2026: Success Patterns from 37 Companies Across Seven Industries," documenting how enterprises are actually deploying generative AI and the outcomes they are achieving.

About the white paper

The white paper analyzes the most repeatable success patterns drawn from the 37 client deployments we have supported to date, organized across seven industries. For each case, we lay out the challenge before adoption, the measures put in place, and the quantitative results delivered.

Industries covered

  • IT & Cloud: company-wide generative AI training and hands-on deployment
  • Chemicals & Materials: bringing AI into R&D processes and new-material ideation
  • Construction & Real Estate: streamlining on-site operations and applying AI to safety management
  • Pharmaceuticals: accelerating data analysis in the drug-discovery process
  • Food & Beverage: improving the accuracy of quality control and demand forecasting
  • Entertainment: streamlining content-production workflows
  • Finance & Insurance: automating the review of compliance documents

Our "context engineering" approach

Across every case, the white paper identifies one common driver of success: the importance of context engineering. Generative AI tools keep getting better by the day, but unless a company's own operational knowledge, decision criteria, and unwritten rules are translated into a form AI can understand, that performance never turns into real results on the ground.

This is precisely the work we specialize in. We have built a track record of supporting 45 companies and 2,500 people — including publicly listed enterprises — in exactly this area. We hope this white paper helps move your own AI adoption forward.
