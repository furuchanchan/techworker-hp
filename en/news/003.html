What We'll Cover

Companies that have adopted generative AI tools yet aren't seeing results tend to share the same patterns. Drawing on our experience supporting 45 companies, this webinar organizes the most common failure patterns into five categories and walks through concrete solutions for each.

  • Failure pattern 1: Spending too much time choosing a tool while neglecting the design of how it's actually used after rollout.
  • Failure pattern 2: Only a handful of AI-literate employees use it, and adoption never spreads across the organization.
  • Failure pattern 3: Experimenting with generic prompts that can't be applied to the company's own specific workflows.
  • Failure pattern 4: Stopping at a satisfying PoC without ever embedding AI into day-to-day operations.
  • Failure pattern 5: Success metrics so vague that ROI can't be explained to leadership.

Who Should Attend

  • You've rolled out ChatGPT or Copilot, but usage rates are low.
  • You're considering a company-wide AI rollout but aren't sure how to approach it.
  • You've completed a PoC but can't see the next step.
  • You need to report the results of your AI investment to leadership.
